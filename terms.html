Section 04

📅 Payment Plans

Go N' Wander offers an optional monthly payment plan for guests who prefer to spread their balance over time. The following terms apply:

How It Works

  • A $150.00 per person deposit (minimum) is due at the time of booking.
  • The remaining balance is divided into equal monthly installments, charged automatically on the 15th of each month.
  • Final payment must clear by April 1, 2027.

Payment Plan Pricing

Guests who elect the monthly payment plan will pay a slightly higher total than the pay-in-full price. This difference accounts for the $65 per person processing fee applied at checkout. The exact total will be disclosed before you confirm.

💡 Pay-in-full guests save on processing fees and lock in their cabin at the lowest available price.

Automatic Charges

By enrolling in a payment plan, you authorize Go N' Wander LLC to automatically charge your payment method on file each month on the 15th until the balance is paid in full. It is your responsibility to ensure your payment method remains valid and has sufficient funds.

Failed Payments

If a scheduled payment fails, Go N' Wander LLC will attempt to reprocess the charge. If the charge is unsuccessful after two (2) attempts, your reservation may be cancelled at our discretion, and all prior payments remain non-refundable. A $35.00 returned payment fee applies to each failed transaction. All balances must clear by April 1, 2027 regardless of payment plan enrollment.
